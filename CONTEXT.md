# rotom.gg

A Pokémon TCG Live player's decks and the logs of the games they played with them.

## Language

**Viewer**:
The player who exported a game log; their draws name the cards.
_Avoid_: Player, user, me

**Opponent**:
The other player in a game log; their draws go unnamed.

**Archetype**:
A named family of decks built around the same Pokémon, as tracked by Limitless (e.g. Dragapult Dusknoir).
_Avoid_: Deck type, meta deck

**Signature Pokémon**:
The one or two Pokémon an Archetype is shown by, in place of its name.
_Avoid_: Mascots, thumbnail

**Substitute**:
What stands in for the Signature Pokémon when the Opponent's Archetype is unknown or "Other".
